FAQ

Straight answers before you start.

Is TaskCash an investment platform?

No. There are no investments and no guaranteed returns. You earn only by completing verified tasks, referring active friends, and joining sponsored campaigns. Packages simply unlock higher daily task limits.

How do deposits work?

Where mobile-money deposits are supported (currently Kenya via M-Pesa STK Push), enter your amount and phone, confirm on your handset, and your balance updates automatically. Deposits fund package purchases; they are not investments.

When can I withdraw?

Once your account is KYC-verified, withdrawals go through a quick admin review (usually within 24h) and are paid via the methods implemented for your country — M-Pesa in Kenya, bank transfer elsewhere. Methods not yet supported are clearly marked "coming soon".

How are tasks verified?

Each task uses one of four methods: automatic verification (videos with watch-time checks), unique codes, screenshot uploads, or manual partner approval. Submissions failing verification are rejected with a reason.

What happens if I cheat?

Fake proofs, emulators, multiple accounts and bot activity are detected and rejected. Repeat abuse means suspension and forfeiture of pending rewards.

Does it cost anything to join?

No — registration is free and includes a welcome bonus. Packages are optional upgrades for higher daily limits.
